# Stage 2 report: dwellA prototype

The first version of the modular kit and of the new geometry nodes (`GN_EdoKitHouse`) is built and tested on dwellA
and its size variants. Everything is generated by the scripts in `tools/kit/`, so it can be rebuilt and adjusted.

## Matches the original

- Grid 1.82 m per bay, 3.14 m storey, posts at every grid point, measured from the original generator with wear switched off.
- Floor = highest ground under the post grid + 1.30 m (same rule as the original; checked by tilting the ground).
- Placed on the 11 original dwellA spots of your village (same position, rotation, terrain object and seed):
  **floor height differs from the original by 6-10 mm on every house.** Footprint and roof sit where they were.
- Stilts, footing stones, engawa posts, steps and fence follow the terrain. On an 8 degree slope the steps
  automatically go from 6 to 12 risers and longer stilt posts are used.
- Roof: 1.21 m overhang, eave 2.78 m above the floor, about 28 degree pitch, corners turned up 0.24 m, as measured.

## Differences from the original (by design)

- Fixed modules: the seed now only picks wall layouts, footing stones and props; it no longer jitters sizes.
- Fence plot is 1 bay larger than the house on the sides and back and 2 bays at the front (the original used about 2.2 m and 3.7 m),
  so fence segments are whole bays. The front fence has an opening in line with the door and steps.
- Real modelled kawara: 7 tile ribs per bay (0.26 m), tile-end edge, ridge and hip caps, onigawara, rafters and soffit boards.

## Textures

10 seamless sets: Timber (rough_wood, stained), Planks, Plaster (warm stucco), Thatch, StoneWall, Moss (photo CC0) and Kawara, Stone,
Paper, Ceramic (procedural). The kawara texture is generated to match the modelled tile ribs exactly (7 ribs x 7 courses per bay),
using only fine grain from the Poly Haven tile scan, because that scan's tiles have the wrong proportions for Japanese roofs.

Delivery size: PNG 475 MB; as JPG 143 MB at 4K or 48 MB at 2K. The repository is about 740 MB, so the 4K set would bring it near
GitHub's recommended 1 GB. Decision needed (see the chat).
